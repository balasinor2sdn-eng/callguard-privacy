Privacy Policy — CallGuard

Last updated: June 26, 2026

p>

CallGuard ("the app") is a call-management app for professionals who receive frequent non-urgent calls. This page explains exactly what data the app accesses, how it's used, and — most importantly — what happens to it.

The short version

All data CallGuard uses stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to a server, sold, or shared with any third party, advertiser, or analytics service. CallGuard does not have a server — it is a fully local, on-device app.

What data CallGuard accesses, and why

td> td> td>
DataWhy it's neededWhere it's stored
Incoming call phone numbers To check whether a number is in your "Always Allow" list, and to detect a repeat call within your chosen bypass window On-device only, in a local app database. Never transmitted anywhere.
SMS sendingTo send your chosen auto-reply text to a caller after a call is screenedSent directly via your phone's SMS service, like any normal text message. CallGuard does not log or store sent message content beyond the message template you wrote yourself in Settings.
Contacts (name + number) Only when you choose to add someone to "Always Allow" using the contact picker On-device only. CallGuard does not read your full contact list in the background — it only accesses a contact when you actively pick one.

What CallGuard does NOT do

  • Does not record calls or call audio
  • Does not upload your contacts, call numbers, or messages to any server
  • Does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs
  • Does not sell or share data with any third party

Data deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you can delete everything CallGuard has stored at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings → Apps → CallGuard → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

CallGuard is intended for working professionals and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy
